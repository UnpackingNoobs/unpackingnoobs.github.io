This is a collection of some special terms and abbreviations that you might find in one of the lections, if you've forgot what they mean, you can look them up here.

| Term        | Meaning          |
|:-------------|:------------------|
| OEP | see _Original Entry Point_ |
| Original Entry Point | After the unpacking is done, this address is called, it's usually the Entry Point of the Original (unpacked) program, hence the name. |
